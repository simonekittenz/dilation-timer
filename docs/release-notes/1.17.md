The screen shows the planned time with the extra time counting up under it, a soft ping and a gentle notification tell you the planned time is up, and the extra time is part of the logged session

- iPhone: the alarm stops the moment you tap **I switched**, and the alarm screen is lighter on older iPhones
- iPhone: a roomier lock screen card with your bunny or kitty and the words on top, and a big clock with a progress bar that fills as the countdown runs. Long dilator names wrap instead of being cut off
